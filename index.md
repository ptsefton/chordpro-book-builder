# ChordPro Songbook Builder

Turns a folder of [ChordPro](https://www.chordpro.org/) song charts and Markdown setlists
into a standalone, interactive, printable songbook — a single HTML file with:

- A song list, with transposition and chord diagrams
- Optional setlists
- Print modes for the whole collection or one setlist, with or without a table of contents
- Chord grids for various instruments, and optional large print
- Floor sheets — song lists and performance notes you can print and put on the floor

## Get started

- [**Build your own songbook**](build/): choose a folder of `.cho`/`.pro` songs and
  `.setlist.md` setlists, give the book a title, and it's saved next to your songs. Needs desktop
  Chrome or Edge. Nothing is uploaded; it all runs in your browser. Use its "Download for offline
  use" link to keep a copy that works without an internet connection.
- [**See a sample songbook**](demo/songbook.html) — built from the files in [`demo/`](demo/).
- [**Download the sample files**](demo/samples.zip) to try building a songbook yourself.
